## Hero & Footer Layout Verification

1. Open the landing page on mobile (~390px) and desktop widths.
2. Scroll to ensure the hero carousel consumes ~85–92% of the viewport; there should be no white gap between the hero section and the footer — the footer becomes visible only after you start scrolling.
3. Watch the carousel advance or use its controls: each slide image stays full-bleed, covering the container without distortion on both phone and desktop.
4. Confirm the text overlays, buttons, and carousel controls remain clickable above the imagery.
5. Repeat the check in Lighthouse/DevTools responsive mode to ensure the footer still renders correctly after the hero and there are no layout shifts when slides change.
